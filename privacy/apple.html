Privacy Policy (Apple App Store)

Effective Date: 08/01/2026

LOC & IVY LLC values your privacy. This policy applies to our applications distributed through the Apple App Store for iOS, iPadOS, and macOS.

1. Data Collection

We adhere to Apple's App Store Review Guidelines concerning user privacy. We minimize data collection and ensure that any data captured is directly related to the app's functionality. We utilize Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework to request your permission before tracking your activity across other companies' apps and websites.

2. Use of Information

Any information collected is used solely to maintain, improve, and provide the features of our applications. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Children's Privacy

Our applications comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple's strict guidelines for apps in the Kids Category. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent.
